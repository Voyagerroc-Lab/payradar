Track rent, loans, bills & subscriptions in one place. No ads, no tracking.

**Full description** (~2,300 characters / max 4000):
```
Take control of every recurring payment — from Netflix and rent to loan installments and post-dated checks — on a single screen. PayRadar is an open-source, privacy-first payment tracker that keeps you ahead of due dates and helps you cut subscription waste.

No ads, no trackers, no data selling. Core payment tracking is free forever.

📊 INSTANT OVERVIEW
• Monthly total and yearly estimate — calculated automatically in your language's currency
• Next payment and active payment count at a glance
• Color-coded countdown badges on every card: due today, 3 days left, overdue
• Filter by category, search by name or notes, sort by amount or date

🏦 11 CATEGORIES — EVERY KIND OF RECURRING PAYMENT
• Rent & housing, utility bills, car & transport
• Bank loan installments with bank name and a 14/60 installment counter
• Check & promissory note due dates with serial number and payee
• Subscriptions, insurance, education, health & fitness, gaming and more

💱 LIVE EXCHANGE RATES — 4 CURRENCIES
• Enter payments in TRY, USD, EUR or MYR
• Rates refresh daily from European Central Bank data
• Totals are calculated in ₺, $ or RM based on your language; the last known rates work offline

🔔 SMART REMINDERS
• Notifications 1–7 days before a due date, plus overdue alerts for up to 2 days
• One digest notification with the grand total when several payments land on the same day
• "Paid / Advance" rolls the due date to the next period in one tap

🎁 FREE-TRIAL TRACKING
• Mark free trials and get warned before your card is charged

🚫 CANCELLATION GUIDES
• Step-by-step guides and direct cancel links for Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, Disney+, Game Pass and 15+ services

📈 PRICE HISTORY
• Old prices are archived automatically when a service raises its fee — see increases on a clear chart

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your data stays on your device by default
• Optional PIN lock encrypts data with AES-256-GCM
• Optional end-to-end encrypted cloud sync: data is encrypted before it leaves your device, so the server can't read it (first 6 months free, then $1/month)
• Open source: github.com/Voyagerroc/payradar

🌍 THREE LANGUAGES, FULLY OFFLINE
• Turkish, English and Malay
• Excel-compatible CSV import/export
• Works completely offline
```

## Play Console → Data Safety Formu Cevapları

| Soru | Cevap |
|---|---|
| Veri topluyor veya paylaşıyor mu? | **Evet, isteğe bağlı** (yalnızca kullanıcı bulut senkronu hesabı açarsa) |
| Toplanan veri türleri | Kişisel bilgiler → **E-posta adresi** (isteğe bağlı: telefon numarası, ad); Finansal bilgiler → **Kullanıcı ödeme kayıtları** (tutar/tarih/ad — kart numarası DEĞİL) |
| Veri şifreli aktarılıyor mu? | **Evet** (HTTPS/TLS) — ayrıca ödeme kayıtları cihazda AES-256-GCM ile şifrelenip öyle yüklenir (uçtan uca) |
| Kullanıcı veri silme talep edebilir mi? | **Evet** — uygulama içinden Hesap > Hesabı Sil (bulut + kimlik) ve Ayarlar > Tüm verileri sil (yerel) |
| Üçüncü taraflarla paylaşım | **Hayır** |
| Reklam amaçlı veri | **Hayır** |
| Veri toplama zorunlu mu? | **Hayır — isteğe bağlı** (uygulama hesapsız tamamen çalışır) |

Not: Bu build'de bulut senkronu kapalıysa (Supabase env yok) form daha da basit: **hiç veri toplanmıyor** seçilebilir. Env'li build yayınlanınca yukarıdaki tabloya geçilmeli.
